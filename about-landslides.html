What is a Landslide?

A landslide is the movement of rock, soil, or debris down a slope, usually triggered by heavy rainfall, steep terrain, or saturated soil.

Common Causes

  • Heavy or prolonged rainfall
  • Steep slope angles
  • High soil moisture / saturation
  • Deforestation and loss of root structure
  • Earthquakes and vibrations

Why It Matters

Landslides can damage property, block roads, and endanger lives with little warning. Monitoring rainfall, slope, and soil conditions helps predict risk before it happens.
